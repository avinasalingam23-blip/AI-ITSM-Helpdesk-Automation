# Business application access requests

Scope: employees need access to a business application or report missing permissions. Owner: Service Desk. Approved demo article, reviewed 2 November 2026.

An access request should identify the application, business reason and required role without including credentials. The Service Desk routes the request to the application owner and manager for approval. Access is granted according to least privilege and the organisation's joiner, mover and leaver process. A model cannot approve permissions or assign a privileged role.

If the application was previously accessible, record when it last worked, the exact error and whether other applications are affected. A password expiry or account lock may require the separate identity recovery workflow. Do not share another employee's account, reuse a colleague's session or attempt to bypass multi-factor authentication. Ask the Service Desk to investigate when the cause is uncertain.

This prototype creates an application entitlement service request for human review. It does not grant permissions, change roles or contact a real identity provider. There is no allowlisted automatic action for application access. The request remains pending until a human reviews it. Request application roles and entitlements through the Service Desk; they are reviewed by the application owner and the employee's manager.
